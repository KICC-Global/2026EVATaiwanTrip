Note Remarks

Fees and Information Changes

The program fees and information are correct at the time of publication but are subject to change due to currency fluctuations and other unforeseen circumstances. In addition, group airfare is only confirmed when the passenger name list is submitted to the airline, so your early commitment is necessary to lock the rate.

Accommodation Costs

Cost is per person based on double occupancy. Triple occupancy rate is the same as double occupancy. Single occupancy requires a single-room supplement fee of USD 1,500.

Payment Methods

The quoted price is offered at a cash discount rate. We accept payments through Zelle or bank transfer. Please remit to the account of KICC. If paying via bank transfer, the full amount must be remitted without deductions.

Travel Insurance

KICC recommends participants purchase additional, more comprehensive travel accidents or health insurance at their own expense. This insurance should cover unforeseen circumstances not included in the limited insurance provided with the program, such as:

  • Trip cancellation
  • Extensive medical emergencies
  • Lost luggage
  • Incidents during independent travel

Participants are responsible for understanding the coverage and terms of any such policy.

Forfeiture of Tour Package Portions

If any traveler forgoes any portion of the tour package, that portion is non-refundable and non-transferable.

  • Clear passport copy for each participant
  • Completed registration form

Cancellation Policy

All cancellations must be in writing, submitted via:

  • Email: jekicc2020@gmail.com (subject line: "Cancellation")
    — Include participant name(s), school, trip departure date, and brief explanation.
  • Postal Mail: Contact for mailing address.

Cancellation Penalties (per person)

Days Before Departure Fee
120+ days Loss of deposit + airline ticket cancellation fees
119–90 days 25% of tour price + airline ticket cancellation fees
89–60 days 50% of tour price + airline ticket cancellation fees
59–31 days 75% of tour price + airline ticket cancellation fees
30 days or less 100% of tour price (no refund)

If your cancellation causes your roommate to pay a single supplement, the additional cost will be deducted from your refund (or added to your balance if unpaid).

Change of Departure Date or Tour

Once your deposit has been processed, any of the following changes will incur the same penalties as cancellation:

  • Change to a different departure date
  • Change to a different tour route
  • Postponement/rescheduling of trip
  • Substitutions: Not allowed
    — A new participant cannot replace a canceled traveler without cancellation penalties.

Uneven Number of Male/Female Participants

If the number of males or females results in an unavoidable single room, a single supplement will be charged.
Alternatives:

  • Triple room at double occupancy rate
  • One single room shared on a rotating basis among group members

Travel Documents

All travelers must have a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the trip's end date.
U.S. citizens may enter Taiwan visa-free.

Room Type

Non-smoking double occupancy rooms (two twin beds) will be provided for the entire trip.
Single rooms available upon request (additional fee applies).

Seating Assignments

Airline seating assignments are made at check-in only (per airline policy).
Participants with medical conditions may contact the airline directly.
