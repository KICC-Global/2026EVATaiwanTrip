### 2) Passport & Visa｜護照與簽證
**Q9 (EN)**: Does my child need a visa to visit Taiwan?  
**A9 (EN)**: U.S. passport holders may stay up to 90 days visa‑free. Passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond entry.

**Q9（繁中）**：是否需要簽證入境臺灣？  
**A9（繁中）**：美國護照持有人可免簽停留最長 90 天；護照效期須自入境日起至少剩餘 6 個月。

**Q10 (EN)**: My child’s passport photo no longer resembles them. Should we renew early?  
**A10 (EN)**: Yes. If appearance no longer matches, apply for a new passport even before expiration.

**Q10（繁中）**：孩子外貌與護照照片差異很大，是否需要提前換發？  
**A10（繁中）**：需要。若外貌與護照照片不符，建議於到期前就先辦理換發。

**Q11 (EN)**: What if my child misses the group flight?  
**A11 (EN)**: Parents must handle rebooking and costs; we’ll assist so the student can rejoin the group in Taiwan.

**Q11（繁中）**：若孩子錯過團體航班怎麼辦？  
**A11（繁中）**：家長需自行負擔改票/補飛費用；我們會協助於臺灣與團隊會合。

### 3) Health & Safety｜健康與安全
**Q12 (EN)**: What if my child gets sick or injured?  
**A12 (EN)**: Minor issues → first aid by teachers. Serious → hospital care with teacher accompaniment, parents contacted immediately, with support from travel insurance.

**Q12（繁中）**：若生病或受傷怎麼處理？  
**A12（繁中）**：輕症由師長提供急救；嚴重狀況立即就醫、老師全程陪同並第一時間聯絡家長，並由旅遊保險協助後續處理。

**Q13 (EN)**: My child has a severe peanut allergy. Can you accommodate?  
**A13 (EN)**: Yes. With advance notice, dining venues will be alerted, an allergy badge can be prepared, and extra care taken at night markets.

**Q13（繁中）**：孩子有嚴重花生過敏，可否配合？  
**A13（繁中）**：可以。若事先告知，我們會通知餐廳、準備過敏識別提示，並於夜市等高風險環境特別留意。

**Q14 (EN)**: Is Taiwan safe?  
**A14 (EN)**: Yes. Taiwan ranks among the world’s safest regions. Measures include buddy system, roll calls, curfews, emergency cards, and adult supervision.

**Q14（繁中）**：臺灣安全嗎？  
**A14（繁中）**：安全。將採夥伴制度、每日點名、門禁、緊急聯絡卡，並由師長全程帶隊。
