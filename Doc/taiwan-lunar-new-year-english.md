# Taiwan Lunar New Year Cultural Experience 7-Day Tour

## Day 1 - 2/15 (Sunday) Taipei Cultural Exploration

### 🗺️ Itinerary
**Morning: Dihua Street Lunar New Year Market**
- **Location**: Datong District, Taipei City, Dihua Street
- **Theme**: Taiwanese Lunar New Year × Traditional Goods × Market Culture
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Festive Atmosphere: Lanterns, spring couplets, and lively crowds create an authentic New Year vibe
  - Traditional Goods: Candies, nuts, dried foods, herbal medicine, and holiday snacks
  - Cultural Tradition: Experience how Taiwanese families prepare for Lunar New Year
  - Photo Spots: Traditional architecture and festive decorations perfect for memorable photos
  - Market Exploration: Walk through stalls to discover New Year foods and goods
  - Tasting Experience: Sample snacks and enjoy the lively interaction with vendors
  - Shopping Fun: Pick souvenirs or gifts while experiencing "New Year shopping"
  - Cultural Learning: Understand the meaning of couplets, red envelopes, and ritual items

**Midday: Taipei Underground Mall**
- **Location**: Zhongzheng District, Taipei City, Taipei Underground Mall (near Taipei Main Station)
- **Theme**: Anime Culture × Youth Trends × Underground Shopping Experience
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Anime Haven: Shops selling anime merchandise, figures, comics, and models
  - Trendy Shopping: Fashion, accessories, and unique items popular among local youth
  - Entertainment Zone: Arcade games, card shops, and fan-made goods
  - Convenient Access: Connected to Taipei Main Station with MRT, HSR, and trains
  - Explore Anime District: Find famous characters and exclusive merchandise
  - Observe Youth Culture: Experience Taiwan's subculture and fashion trends
  - Shopping Fun: Buy souvenirs, quirky items, or snacks
  - Free Interaction: Enjoy the lively hangout spot for Taiwanese students

**Afternoon: Taipei 101 Skyscraper**
- **Location**: Xinyi District, Taipei City, Taipei 101
- **Theme**: Reach the Top, View the World
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - 89th Floor Observatory: Panoramic views of Taipei, stunning by day or night
  - Wind Damper: A massive golden sphere engineering marvel that stabilizes the tower against typhoons and earthquakes
  - World Landmark: Taiwan's most iconic skyscraper, blending modern design with cultural symbolism
  - High-Speed ElThe Meadows Schooltor: Ride to the 89th floor in just 37 seconds
  - Multilingual Audio Guide: Learn the stories behind landmarks and Taipei's cityscape
  - Photography: Capture breathtaking views of Taipei Basin and surrounding mountains
  - Souvenir Shopping: Browse unique Taipei 101 collectibles at the observatory gift shop

## Day 3 - 2/17 (Tuesday) Hakka Culture Deep Dive

### 🗺️ Itinerary
**Morning: Qing'an Tofu Street & Chuanlong Old House Tofu Workshop**
- **Location**: Wufeng, Hsinchu
- **Theme**: Century-old House, Fragrance of Tofu
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Chuanlong Old House: A well-preserved Qing Dynasty residence with historic architecture
  - Tofu Street: A food street famous for a wide variety of tofu dishes
  - Cultural Details: Experience the blend of heritage and culinary culture inside an old house
  - Tofu DIY: Learn the traditional process of grinding soybeans, pressing, and shaping tofu
  - Tofu Tasting Trio: Try three different cooking styles—fried, braised, and steamed
  - Douhua Dessert: Enjoy smooth and delicate traditional tofu pudding
  - Cultural Tour: Discover the history and local stories of Qing'an Tofu Street

**Afternoon: Oil-Paper Umbrella Workshop**
- **Location**: Miaoli County
- **Theme**: Discover the beauty of traditional Taiwanese crafts by hand-painting your own oil-paper umbrella
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Traditional Craft: The oil-paper umbrella symbolizes blessings and protection, reflecting Hakka and Minnan cultural heritage
  - Workshop Ambience: Learn about the intricate handmade process and cultural craftsmanship
  - Creative Art: Combine tradition and creativity to create a one-of-a-kind piece
  - Learn the history and making process of oil-paper umbrellas
  - Paint your own umbrella under the guidance of instructors
  - Take home your finished umbrella as a meaningful souvenir

**Afternoon: Strawberry Farms**
- **Location**: Dahu Township, Miaoli County
- **Theme**: Enjoy Taiwan's seasonal fruit culture through hands-on strawberry picking
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Strawberry Capital: Dahu is Taiwan's most famous strawberry-growing region
  - Seasonal Charm: Best enjoyed in winter to early spring, with festive farm scenery
  - Fresh Harvest: Picked strawberries are sweet, juicy, and farm-fresh
  - Enter the strawberry fields and pick ripe strawberries by hand
  - Taste strawberry desserts or ice treats (e.g., strawberry mochi, strawberry ice cream)
  - Learn about strawberry farming and local agricultural traditions

**Evening: Woodcarving Workshop**
- **Location**: Sanyi Township, Miaoli County
- **Theme**: Hands-on experience of Taiwan's woodcarving art and traditional craftsmanship
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Woodcarving Hub: Sanyi is renowned as Taiwan's "Woodcarving Capital" with rich artistic heritage
  - Cultural Depth: Designs often reflect religion, nature, and everyday life
  - Workshop Setting: Feel the aroma of wood and the focus of local artisans
  - Learn the history of woodcarving and Sanyi's cultural background
  - Create your own simple carving or painting under instructor guidance (e.g., face mask, wooden plaque)
  - Take home your handmade artwork as a unique souvenir

## Day 5 - 2/19 (Thursday) Ancient Capital Culture & Hot Spring Experience

### 🗺️ Itinerary
**Morning: Confucius Temple**
- **Location**: West Central District, Tainan
- **Theme**: Visit Taiwan's first Confucius Temple and experience Confucian cultural heritage
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Historical Significance: Built in 1665, the first Confucius Temple in Taiwan, known as the "First Academy of Taiwan"
  - Architectural Beauty: Features the elegant Dacheng Hall, Pan Pond, and ceremonial archways
  - Cultural Symbol: A key site for Confucian worship and the starting point of Taiwan's education tradition
  - Tour Dacheng Hall and Pan Pond to learn about Confucian rituals
  - Appreciate architectural details reflecting Confucian values
  - Stroll through surrounding historic streets for an immersive cultural atmosphere

**Midday: Fuzhong Creative Street**
- **Location**: West Central District, Tainan
- **Theme**: Stroll through a creative old street where history meets innovation
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Historic Charm: Red-brick lanes and traditional houses reflecting Tainan's cultural heritage
  - Youthful Creativity: Local young designers showcasing originality in small shops
  - Diverse Mix: From traditional crafts to modern handmade products blending past and present
  - Explore creative boutiques and find unique souvenirs
  - Taste local snacks and specialty drinks
  - (Optional, self-funded) Dress in traditional costumes and take photos around the Confucius Temple and old street

**Midday: Fucheng Banquet Restaurant**
- **Location**: West Central District, Tainan
- **Theme**: Enjoy state banquet-style cuisine blending Tainan's history and culinary creativity
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Banquet Heritage: Fucheng has hosted multiple state banquets, showcasing Taiwan's hospitality
  - Local Ingredients: Featuring Tainan's seafood, fruits, and regional produce in refined dishes
  - Cultural Essence: Menu design reflects Tainan's heritage while fusing tradition with innovation
  - Taste multiple courses of banquet-level cuisine inspired by history and local culture
  - Experience Taiwan's banquet dining etiquette and hospitality traditions
  - Enjoy the elegant atmosphere that blends classical charm with modern style

**Afternoon: Guanziling Hot Spring Area & Water and Fire Cave**
- **Location**: Baihe District, Tainan
- **Theme**: Discover Taiwan's unique geothermal wonders and rejuvenating hot springs
- **Featured Experiences**:
  - Mud Hot Spring: The only gray-black mud hot spring in Taiwan, rich in minerals for health and beauty benefits
  - Water and Fire Cave: A rare phenomenon where natural gas and spring water coexist, creating "fire burning on water"
  - Nature & Wellness: Combines geological wonders with a relaxing spa experience
  - Relax with a mud hot spring bath or foot soak
  - Visit the Water and Fire Cave to witness this rare geothermal spectacle
  - Capture photos of the natural phenomenon as a memorable travel highlight
